# Sources and third-party notices

## Initial historical records

Tony Jaques, *Dictionary of Battles and Sieges: A Guide to 8,500 Battles from Antiquity through the Twenty-first Century*, volumes 1–3, Greenwood Press, 2007. The pilot stores extracted facts, source page references, and brief new paraphrases. The PDF and full entry prose are not included. No license to the original book is granted by this repository.

## Coordinates

Initial reference coordinates were retrieved from Wikipedia GeoData and Wikidata P625 on 2026-11-02. Per-event URLs identify the sources. Gazetteer coordinates are provisional matches, not historically verified battlefield positions. Wikipedia content and Wikidata structured data have different terms; consult the linked sources and [Wikidata's data access policy](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access). No bulk Wikipedia article text is included.
